# Overseas AI Investment and Capital Markets Sources

This file defines the preferred sources for tracking overseas AI investment, public markets, primary markets, M&A, financing, and capital allocation signals. This section is for market intelligence only, not investment advice.

## Core Principle

Investment-related content must prioritize primary documents and high-quality financial sources.

- Public markets: start with company investor relations, SEC filings, earnings releases, exchange notices, and management commentary.
- Primary markets: start with company announcements, investor announcements, SEC Form D, and reputable deal databases/media.
- Do not provide buy/sell recommendations or price targets.
- Separate facts, market signals, and interpretation.
- Clearly label uncertain information as unconfirmed or media-reported.

## Source Priority for Public Markets

- P0: SEC EDGAR filings, company investor relations, earnings releases, annual reports, quarterly reports, 8-K, S-1, investor presentations, official press releases.
- P1: Nasdaq, NYSE, company filings, earnings call transcripts, official exchange announcements.
- P2: Reuters, Bloomberg, Financial Times, Wall Street Journal, CNBC, Barron's, MarketWatch.
- P3: SemiAnalysis, The Information, industry research, sell-side summaries, and specialist newsletters as secondary context.
- P4: Social media and community discussion only as discovery signals.

## Source Priority for Primary Markets

- P0: Company financing announcements, investor/VC official announcements, SEC Form D, official M&A announcements.
- P1: Reuters, Bloomberg, Financial Times, Wall Street Journal, The Information.
- P2: Crunchbase, PitchBook, CB Insights, Dealroom, Tracxn where accessible.
- P3: Founder posts, LinkedIn, X/Twitter, newsletters, and podcasts only as leads requiring verification.

## Primary Market Observation Themes

Track fundraising, strategic investments, M&A, and IPO signals across:

- Foundation model companies: OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, Mistral, Cohere, Perplexity, and comparable players.
- AI agent companies: enterprise agents, coding agents, browser agents, workflow agents, personal assistants.
- AI infrastructure: vector databases, RAG, MCP tools, model deployment, GPU cloud, inference optimization.
- Data center and compute leasing: GPU cloud, AI data centers, power-linked compute deals.
- Vertical AI applications: legal, finance, healthcare, education, customer service, sales, marketing, office automation.
- Strategic investment and M&A: cloud-model partnerships, acquihires, supply-chain investments, model distribution agreements.
- IPO signals: S-1 filings, governance changes, revenue scale signals, secondary offerings, major valuation resets.

## Reporting Rules

For each investment item, capture:

1. Market segment: chips, cloud, software, data centers, energy, AI applications, model labs, agent platforms, or infrastructure.
2. Event type: earnings, filing, financing, M&A, IPO signal, strategic partnership, capex, valuation change, or market rotation.
3. Primary source if available.
4. What changed?
5. Why it matters for AI industry evolution.
6. Key risks or uncertainties.
7. Do not include buy/sell advice.

## Avoid

- Pure stock price movement without industry logic.
- Funding-only news with no strategic relevance.
- Unverified valuation rumors without clear source labeling.
- Generic market commentary unrelated to AI infrastructure, products, regulation, or adoption.
